# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Rewritten from scratch: one small core, one plugin, one worker.
- Internet rooms over WebRTC with Opus at 510 kbps; lossless on a LAN.
- Anyone can listen in a browser at relay.matari-audio.com/<room>.
- The buffer sizes itself to the network and resyncs in silence.
- IN/OUT meters with true peak, and an output fader.
